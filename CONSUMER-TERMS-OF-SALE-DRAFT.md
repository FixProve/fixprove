## 4. Your 14-day right of withdrawal (fortrydelsesret)

You have the right to withdraw from this purchase within 14 days without giving any
reason, under the Danish Consumer Contracts Act (Forbrugeraftaleloven) and Directive
2011/83/EU. The withdrawal period expires 14 days from the day the contract is entered
into.

To exercise this right, tell us clearly (e.g. email to yehor@yehor.ai) that you wish to
withdraw, using the model form in `EU-MODEL-WITHDRAWAL-FORM.md` or your own words. If you
withdraw before we have started delivering the service, you get a full refund. If
delivery has already started at your request (see §5), different rules apply.

## 5. Starting the service before the 14 days end — and what that costs you

If you want access immediately rather than waiting 14 days, we will ask for your express
consent to that at checkout, and separately ask you to acknowledge what it costs you.
[COUNSEL-REVIEW: the precise legal effect for a continuous/subscription SERVICE — as

## 6. Conformity — what you're entitled to expect

The service must match its description and be fit for its stated purpose. If it doesn't,
you're entitled to have it brought into conformity, a price reduction, or (in serious
cases) to end the contract — under the Danish rules implementing Directive (EU)
2019/770. This is in addition to, not instead of, the disclaimers in the main Terms of
Service about FixProve being a verification aid rather than a correctness guarantee —
those disclaimers describe what the tool does, not an attempt to contract out of
mandatory conformity rights.

§4–5 apply instead. See `REFUND-CANCELLATION-POLICY-DRAFT.md` for the full mechanics.
