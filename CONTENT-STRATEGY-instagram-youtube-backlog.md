## The idea

A third, distinct content channel — separate from the existing
LinkedIn/Facebook #buildinpublic register (numeric hook, program-credit,
honest gap, aphorism, concrete next step), which speaks to an investor/
funding audience.

- **Audience:** general public, not investors or Danish B2B buyers —
  people feeling stuck in specific, nameable ways.
- **Pain points to connect with (Yehor's own framing):**
  - Student disillusionment with education — the sense of spending years
    learning things that won't be relevant in 5-10 years.
  - Difficulty finding work after graduation despite that investment.
  - Loss of autonomy inside corporate culture.
  - A broader loss of sense/meaning in life.
- **Format:** educational, entertaining Instagram content built around
  these pain points, funneling traffic to YouTube tutorials that show,
  step by step, how to build something like FixProve (or other tools).
- **Funnel direction:** Instagram (pain-point hooks, discovery) →
  YouTube (tutorial depth, demonstration of the product in the process
  of being built).

## Why deferred, not dropped

Running three distinct audiences at once — funding/investors,
Danish B2B customers, and this broader consumer audience — is a real
bandwidth question for a solo founder, not a strategy question. The
next nine days carry two live events (Sept 16, Sept 17) framed, per
Travis's advice, as places small Danish customers might actually be
standing in the room, plus two still-open P0 product defects (stale
`--version` string, inconsistent CLI grammar between the pip and npm
distributions) that should be fixed before either event. This idea
competes for the same hours.

The idea itself is sound: a "build FixProve" tutorial series is a
natural, non-salesy way to put the product in front of exactly the
developers who'd use it, and pain-point-driven Instagram hooks are a
proven format. It's filed here specifically so it survives past
Sept 17 rather than getting lost.
