**Correction to a common template default, verified this session (2026-08-05):** do
**not** include a link to the EU's "Online Dispute Resolution" (ODR) platform
(ec.europa.eu/consumers/odr). It was formally discontinued — fully shut down 20 July
2025, all case data deleted, under Regulation (EU) 2024/3228 — and EU guidance now
explicitly requires businesses to **remove**, not add, any reference to it from terms and
websites. Its replacement is a new digital tool that will route consumers to national ADR
bodies once the revised ADR Directive (in force since 19 January 2026) is transposed into
Danish law — not yet available at the time of this draft. Do not add an ODR link to any
FixProve page; if a specific EU/Danish ADR body reference is wanted once one exists,
that's a fresh lookup at the time, not this draft.
Sources: [Bird & Bird](https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2025/global/the-end-of-the-european-online-dispute-resolution-platform),
[iubenda](https://www.iubenda.com/en/help/173458).
