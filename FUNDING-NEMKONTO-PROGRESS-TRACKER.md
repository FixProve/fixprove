## 1. NemKonto (business bank account) — precondition for any grant payout

**Status: OPEN.** `[PROJECT RECORD]` CVR 46646223 has no NemKonto
registered (confirmed 2026-08-04, `virk.nemkonto.dk` screenshot). A
personal account cannot serve as a CVR's NemKonto — a business bank
account is required first, then linked as NemKonto. Public grants
(including both programs below) pay out to NemKonto, so this blocks
payout even if an application is approved — it does not block *applying*.

### Option comparison — double-checked 2026-09-02, live pages + official nemkonto.dk

There are two genuinely different paths to a NemKonto, not one. Route A
(a Danish-licensed bank) is the simple, standard path. Route B (a foreign
institution) is officially supported too — confirmed directly on
`nemkonto.dk`'s own "NemKonto in a Foreign Financial Institution" page
(updated 23 Feb 2026) — but goes through a separate application to the
Danish Agency for Digital Government, either via MitID Erhverv (added as
an option, per that same page) or a paper form. An early search pass
wrongly concluded foreign accounts (Wise, Revolut) simply "cannot" be a
NemKonto — corrected here after checking the official page directly.

Lunar Business (Simple)** | 0 kr setup, **~289 kr/mo** (2,890 kr/yr) per most recent source — one older source said 241 kr/mo, so treat as approximate | Standard domestic route (Danish-licensed bank, Danish IBAN) | Fast, routine | `[SEARCH ONLY — lunar.app is blocked in this session's browser, could not verify on the live page; price should be confirmed directly before committing]` |

2. Describe the founder's and/or team's motivation, competencies,

   realistic ambition backed by a concrete plan with defined initiatives

5. Describe the immediate competitive situation and market — particular
   focus on elements of innovation and new value added to the market.

**Scoring, verbatim:** three criteria, 0–10 points each, minimum **22/30**
to be admitted.
- *Forudsætninger* (prerequisites): tested business model adding new

  relevant experience/education/track record; access to network/advisory
  board.
- *Ambitioner og vækstplan* (ambitions/growth plan): clear growth
  ambitions; concrete realistic plan with milestones; ability to execute.
- *Behov for kompetenceudvikling/rådgivning* (advisory need): how clearly
  and specifically the need is described; whether it ties to the growth
  plan; whether the advisory can unlock/support the growth.
