# Addendum — vFault pricing correction (2026-09-11)

**Does not edit `FixProve-vFault-Legal-Diligence.pdf`** — per this project's
append-only convention for reports, corrections land as a dated addendum,
not an edit to the original document.

## What the diligence PDF states

`FixProve-vFault-Legal-Diligence.pdf` (its own "Sources and evidence notes"
section dates the vFault homepage check to **9 September 2026**) records
vFault's advertised tiers as:

- Pro — £19/month
- Team — £49/month
- Business — £99/month

## Independent re-check, 2026-09-11 (executor session)

Fetched `https://vfault.com/` directly and read the pricing section. As of
this check, the live tiers are:

- Free — £0
- Pro — £9.99/month (1 developer)
- Team — £29.99/month (up to 5 developers)
- Business — £49.99/month (up to 15 developers)

This matches the correction a guide-chat session (2026-09-09/11) had
already flagged from its own re-check, now independently confirmed by the
executor via a direct fetch rather than taken on trust.

## What this does and doesn't mean

The two-day (or less) gap between the PDF's own dated check and this one
is notable but not itself alarming — a small SaaS can change pricing at
will, and nothing else in the diligence PDF's analysis (controller
identity, legal-surface gaps, the G1-G7 launch architecture, the 22
lawyer-question answers) depends on the exact price points. This addendum
exists so nobody cites the PDF's £19/£49/£99 figures as current going
forward. If vFault's pricing is cited again in FixProve materials, use
£9.99/£29.99/£49.99 and re-verify at time of use — per the diligence PDF's
own evidence caveat, page surfaces change.
