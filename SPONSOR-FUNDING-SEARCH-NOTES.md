## 2. What it actually asks for — NOT a lump-sum ask

This is the single most important fact for framing next session's
"sponsor search." The canvas is explicit and disciplined about what it is
and isn't requesting:

**Asked for:**
- Legal costs, "small, concrete": **DKK 6,000–10,000** for the
  privacy/terms review, deferred-payment terms under discussion.
- **Living runway** (framed as the real constraint, not capital): a few
  months of focused founder time — this is time/income support, not an
  equity or grant check.
- **GTM guidance**: experienced advice on direct B2B sales to
  Danish/Nordic software teams.
- **Network access**: warm introductions to 10–50-person, AI-heavy
  software teams.

**Explicitly NOT asked for:** product-development funding (already
built), infrastructure (near-zero cost), or hiring (premature).

**Implication for next session:** "finding sponsors for FixProve" should
not default to a generic funding/investor search. The canvas itself
frames the ask narrowly — small legal-cost help, time/runway support,
GTM mentorship, and warm intros — closer to incubator/mentor-network
territory than investor outreach. Confirm with Yehor whether that framing
still holds before broadening scope.
